01 JPBD Housing Guidelines Bahagian A + PR1–PR7 · Edisi Kelima 2025 23 Q&As ▾
PR1 · Housing GP Edisi Kelima 2025
Minimum lot size for a terrace house (rumah teres)?
6.10 m × 18.29 m (20' x 60')
Min built-up floor area 74.32 m² (800 sf); minimum 3 bedrooms; max 3 storeys / 16.76 m (55'); floor-to-floor max 4.27 m (14').
PR1 · Housing GP
Setback requirements for a terrace house?
Front 6.10 m (20') · Corner side 6.10 m · Car porch column to boundary 3.05 m (10')
Rear lane 4.57 m (15'); separator lane between blocks 6.10 m — property break NOT allowed; corner cut 4.57 x 4.57 m; service road min 15.24 m (50').
PR1 · Housing GP
Maximum height, storeys and block length for terrace houses?
Max 3 storeys, 16.76 m (55') · Block length max 97.54 m (320')
Measured from access road level to roof; floor-to-floor max 4.27 m; service road min 15.24 m; rear lane 4.57 m; separator lane 6.10 m no property break.
PR1 · PR2 · PR3 · Housing GP
Cul-de-sac (jalan buntu) limits for landed housing?
Terrace: max 97.54 m (320') · Semi-D / cluster / bungalow: max 304.80 m (1,000')
Turning head min 15.24 x 15.24 m (50' x 50'); radius 7.62 m (25'). Applies to PR1, PR2, PR3 landed schemes.
PR2 · Housing GP
Minimum lot size for semi-detached (berkembar) and cluster houses?
Semi-D 9.14 × 18.29 m (30' x 60') · Cluster 10.67 × 19.81 m (35' x 65')
Cluster = 4 units per cluster sharing rear walls. Min floor area 74.32 m² / 81.29 m² (875 sf); 3 bedrooms; max 3 storeys / 16.76 m.
PR2 · Housing GP
Setback for semi-D and cluster houses?
Front 6.10 m (20') · Corner 6.10 m · End-lot side 3.05 m (10')
Cul-de-sac up to 304.80 m (1,000'); pedestrian path 1.83 m (6') encouraged on both sides of the front; car porch column 3.05 m.
PR3 · Housing GP
Minimum lot size and setbacks for a bungalow (sesebuah)?
Lot 15.24 × 22.86 m (50' x 75') · Front 6.10 m · Rear 3.05 m · Side 3.05 m · Corner 6.10 m
Min floor area 125.42 m² (1,350 sf); 3 bedrooms; max 3 storeys / 16.76 m; car porch column to boundary 3.05 m; cul-de-sac up to 304.80 m.
PR4 · Housing GP
Strata landed housing (strata bertanah) minimum requirements?
Min 50 units · 1 lot / 2 owners · Max 4 storeys / 21.34 m (70') · Lot 6.10 × 18.29 m
Access opening min 20.12 m (66') with 2 entries/exits; min floor area 74.32 m² (800 sf); floor-to-floor max 4.27 m; block max 97.54 m.
PR4 · Housing GP
Internal roads and fencing for strata landed schemes?
Road 12.19 m (1-way) / 15.24 m (2-way) incl. reserve · Fence max 2.74 m (9') with min 33% open
Fence must remain visible from outside. Parking 2 TLK/unit + 20% visitor; OKU bays 2% with ramp; visitor parking within guarded area.
PR4 · Housing GP
Shared facilities for strata landed schemes (PR4)?
Tadika/taska 55.74–74.32 m² by unit count · Surau 46.45–55.74 m² · Office 46.45 m² · Dewan 92.90–185.81 m²
>400 units: tadika and surau +0.186 m² per additional unit. Guardhouse min 46.45 m² (500 sf).
PR5 · Housing GP 2025 (new)
Rumah Kluster Berkembar (PR5) requirements?
8 units per cluster · 1 lot / 2 owners · 2 storeys / 15.24 m (50') max
Corner lot 13.72 × 14.02 m (45' x 46'), intermediate 4.88–6.10 × 16.76–18.90 m; floor area 84.54–86.96 m² (910–936 sf); private garden for all units; new type in 5th Ed.
PR6 · Housing GP
Apartment / condominium maximum density (harga bebas)?
80 units/acre (bandaraya & perbandaran) · 60 units/acre (daerah)
Plinth max 60% of site; min floor area 60.39 m² (650 sf); min 3 bedrooms not required — per unit type table.
PR6 · Housing GP
Apartment height limits — storeys and podium?
Max 5 storeys without lift · Podium max 8 storeys from access road level
Ground level priority: OKU units, warga emas, tadika/taska, surau. Service road 20.12 m (66'); exit road 15.24 m; internal road 6.10 m (1-way) / 7.32 m (2-way).
PR6 · Housing GP
Building separation between apartment blocks?
Front–front 24.38 m (80') · Side–front / side–rear / rear–rear 12.19 m · Side–side 9.14 m (30')
Facing public road 12.19 m; building to boundary 6.10 m; basement 3.05 m. No balcony or ramp within the setback zone.
PR6 · Housing GP
Parking requirement for apartments / condominiums?
2 TLK per unit + 20% visitor · TLM 20% of TLK · OKU 2% with ramp
Tandem parking allowed per GP Infrastruktur. EVCB mandatory if >50 units: min 1% of TLK incl. 1 OKU-accessible bay.
PR6 · Housing GP
Community facilities for apartments (PR6)?
Tadika/taska: 200 units = 55.74 m², 250–400 = 74.32 m², >400 = +0.186 m²/unit · Surau 46.45–55.74 m² · Warga emas room 72.46–232.26 m²
Jenazah room beside surau; non-Islamic prayer ≥50 units 7.43 m², >400 units meditation room 46.45 m²; mail bay min 2.13 m wide; management office 46.45 m²; dewan 92.90–185.81 m².
Bahagian A 6.1 · also PR6/PR7
Open space (tanah lapang) requirement for housing?
10% of land (≥50 units or ≥5 acres) · 5% (<50 units) — whichever is higher
Min 70% active recreation; ≥50% of that on flat open ground, centred; up to 50% may be inside building (podium/roof); max 30% non-recreation (planting, buffer, pond, utility).
Bahagian A 6.2 · IND7
Guardhouse (pondok pengawal) standards?
Landed: max 1.83 × 2.44 m (6' x 8'), set back ≥6.10 m (20') from road, barrier clearance ≥4.57 m (15')
Strata: may integrate with main building / open roof; combine with water meter, TNB meter, refuse chamber. Industrial MIP guardhouse same size (IND7).
Bahagian A 6.3 · GEN-L commercial
Perimeter planting widths?
3.05 m (10') facing main road · 1.52 m (5') shared boundary
Width counts within the building setback. Developments >1 acre; <1 acre: 1.52 m all around. Only 30% of perimeter planting counts toward open space.
Bahagian A 6.4 · Housing GP
Community facilities provision — landed vs strata?
Landed: site reserve (rizab tapak) · Strata: integrated building space
Per Garis Panduan Kemudahan Masyarakat Negeri Selangor (KM2-4-GP).
Bahagian A 5.2 · Housing GP
Housing type definitions?
Teres: row ≥3 units, shared walls · Berkembar: 2 units, one shared wall · Kluster: 4 units/cluster · Kluster Berkembar: 8 units · Sesebuah: standalone · Bandar: ≤4 storeys strata
Pangsapuri/kondominium: complete stacked units with facilities. Strata: parcels + accessory parcels + common property (SMA 2013).
PR7 · Housing GP
Rumah Selangorku terrace and rumah bandar (PR7) standards?
Teres: as PR1 · Rumah Bandar strata: max 4 storeys / 21.34 m (70'), 1 lot / 2 owners
3 bedrooms min; separator lane 6.10 m no property break; block max 97.54 m; service road 15.24 m; lot size per Dasar RSku 3.0.
PR7 · Housing GP
Selangorku apartment density and parking (PR7)?
Density 120 units/acre (Selangorku only, all zones) · 90 u/a mixed with free market (5–10 ac)
Parking: Solo/Duo/Trio 1 TLK/unit + 20% visitor; Quad 2 TLK + 10%; TLM 1 per 2 units; EVCB mandatory >50 units, 1% TLK.
02 JPBD Commercial Planning Guidelines PN1–PN20 · Edisi Kelima 2025 21 Q&As ▾
PN1 · Commercial GP 2025
Shop house (kedai teres) minimum lot size and block length?
Lot 6.10 × 18.29 m (20' x 60') · Max block 54.86 m (180') · Max 5 storeys
Block length cut from 320' in the 4th Ed. Verandah way 1.52 m (5'). Second staircase required from 3 storeys.
PN1 · Commercial GP
Service road and rear lanes for shop blocks?
Service road 15.24 m (1–2 storeys) / 20.12 m (3–5 storeys) · Shop rear lane 6.10 m / 9.14 m
Residential rear lane 4.57 m; 1.50 m separating wall between shop and residential rear lanes; TKL parallel to 30' rear lane allowed.
PN2 · Commercial GP
Affordable shophouse (kedai teres mampu milik)?
Min floor area 65.03 m² (700 sf) / unit · Max block 97.54 m (320') · Max 3 storeys
Verandah way 1.52 m; parking as PN1; quota subject to Selangor state policy and circulars.
PN3 · Commercial GP
Twin shop / individual shop (kedai berkembar / sesebuah) lot sizes?
Berkembar 18.29 × 39.64 m (60' x 130') · Sesebuah 27.43 × 39.62 m (90' x 130')
Service road 20.12 m; front setback 12.19 m; side/back 6.10 m; max 5 storeys; separate loading bay; centralised landscaped waste collection.
Bahagian A · Commercial GP 2025
Commercial setbacks (general)?
Front 12.19 m (40') · Side & rear 6.10 m (20') · Basement 3.05 m (10')
Service road min 15.24 m two-way with min 2 exits, no cul-de-sac; slip road min 15 m; access not directly onto roads with ≥20 m reserve.
Bahagian A · all PN types
Commercial parking rates (TLK / TLM)?
TLK 1 per 46.45 m² (500 sf) net floor + 20% visitor · TLM 1 per 92.90 m² (1,000 sf)
TLKW (women) 2% of TLK; TLK OKU 2% near entrance/lift with ramp; e-hailing bays 10% of TLM, clearly marked and accessible.
Bahagian A · all PN types
EVCB (EV charging bays) requirement for commercial developments?
Mandatory if >50 TLK · Min 1% of TLK · Min 1 OKU-accessible unit
Applies across all PN types. Tandem parking allowed subject to GP Infrastruktur Negeri Selangor.
PN6 · Commercial GP
Hotel parking requirement?
TLK 1 per 3 hotel rooms · TLB (bus) 1 per 100 rooms + drop-off layby
Support spaces per commercial floor area; plinth max 60%; front setback 12.19 m; in-site drop-off for bus, taxi, e-hailing.
PN7 · Commercial GP
Hypermarket / supermarket minimum site and retail mix?
Min site 1.8 ha (4.5 acres) · Hypermarket floor >5,000 m² · Support retail 40–80% · Warehouse ≥10%
Halal/non-halal separation for loading, waste, cold storage; medan selera 5% of net floor; trolley storage inside building + parking areas.
PN8 · Commercial GP
Petrol station (stesen minyak) requirements?
Site 0.40 ha (with service) / 0.20 ha (self-service) · Min 60.96 m (200') from junction
EVCB min 2 bays (1 OKU); fuel tank setback 3.05 m; NGV 200 m from housing; ingress/egress 15.24 m / 9.14 m; corner cut ≤45°; no station opposite unless road divided.
PN12 · Commercial GP
SOHO requirements?
Floor 41.81–51.10 m² (450–550 sf), max 2 occupants/unit · TLK 1/unit + 40% visitor
Podium max 8 storeys; open space 5% (≤1.21 ha) / 10% (>1.21 ha); support retail ≥8% of net floor; TLM 20% of TLK; not in low-density residential areas.
PN13 · Commercial GP (new 5th Ed)
Serviced apartment (pangsapuri perkhidmatan) requirements?
Floor >51.10 m² (550 sf) · TLK 2/unit + 20% visitor (51.10–92.90 m²), +1 TLK per extra 46.45 m²
Max occupants 3–4 by size; podium 8 storeys; plinth 60%; meditation room >400 units (46.45 m²); commercial space ≥8%; EVCB >50 units; tandem allowed.
PN14 · Commercial GP (new)
Private hospital (hospital swasta) requirements?
Site ≥0.40 ha (1 acre) · TLK 1 per 46.45 m² + 20% visitor + 20% additional · Ambulance bay min 1
Surau, visitor lobby, ambulance/emergency layby, taxi/e-hailing waiting area; landscape 5%; EVCB >50 TLK. New in 5th Ed.
PN15 · Commercial GP (new)
Private school (rendah / menengah) parking and site?
TLK 1 per 2 staff + 20% visitor · TLM 20% of TLK · TLB min 1 · Site ≥0.40 ha
Bus layby min 3.66 m wide; covered bicycle parking; sports field, canteen, library, surau; perimeter fencing. New in 5th Ed.
PN16 · Commercial GP (new)
IPTS (private higher education) parking?
Academic 1 TLK/46.45 m² · Admin 1/46.45 m² + 10% · Hostel 1/92.90 m² + 10%
Site ≥0.40 ha; TLB min 1; open space 10% (>1.21 ha) / 5%; bus layby 3.66 m. New in 5th Ed.
PN17 · Commercial GP (new)
Theme park (taman tema) limits?
Built-up max 40% of site · Landscape 10%
4 classifications: individual, in-building, integrated, replica. Emergency access required; bus/taxi layby outside development; EVCB min 1% TLK. New in 5th Ed.
PN18 · Commercial GP (new)
Golf course (padang golf) minimum land and parking?
9-hole ≥30.35 ha (75 ac) · 18-hole ≥60.70 ha (150 ac) · 2 TLK per hole
Clubhouse 1 TLK/49.98 m²; driving range 1/49.98 m² + 10%; fairway setbacks 29.87–60.04 m; permitted zones include pelancongan, pertanian, brownfield. New in 5th Ed.
PN20 · Commercial GP (new)
Sports complex (kompleks sukan) site areas and parking?
Stadium 3.00 ha · Complex 1.20 ha · Pool / field 1.50 ha · Squash & tennis 1.00 ha · TLK 1 per 8 seats
Courts 1 TLK per 20 m² court floor; commercial 1/500 sf; TLB 1 per 1,000 spectators (<40,000), 1 per 2,000 above. New in 5th Ed.
Common · all PN types
Surau sizes for commercial buildings?
≤50,000 sf GFA: 6.10 × 6.10 m · 50,001–100,000 sf: 6.10 × 10.67 m · >100,000 sf: 6.10 × 15.24 m (each men & women)
Qiblat orientation; separate men/women with ablution; NOT allowed on rooftop, basement, parking, loading bay, facing toilets or below sewage pipes.
PN9 · PN10 · PN11 · Commercial GP
Bazar, arked, R&R and medan selera standards?
Bazar site 0.10 ha, stall 9.57–12.63 m², max 3 storeys · R&R max 4 storeys, surau 6.10 × 6.10 m each · Medan selera site 0.20 ha
R&R needs ATM + wakaf; medan selera not facing residential rows, TLK 1/46.45 m² + 50% customer, side/rear 3.05 m.
Commercial GP 2025 · Bahagian A
Priority of standards when documents conflict?
1st LPHS Pekeliling Bil. 2/2016 · 2nd GP Perniagaan Negeri Selangor 2025 · 3rd JPBD/PLANMalaysia national guidelines
Plot ratio per RT/RKK; excludes podium/basement parking; measured between ukur/semadan boundary lines.

Industrial GP 2025 · buffer zon
Industrial buffer zones to residential?
Heavy 300 m (planting 20.12 m) · Medium 150 m (10.06 m) · Light 50 m (10.06 m)
Measured from building setback line of the industrial lot to building setback line of the nearest residential lot.
IND1 · Industrial GP 2025
Industrial terrace factory lot size?
6.10 × 24.38 m (20' x 80') · Affordable industrial terrace 6.10 × 18.29 m (20' x 60')
Block max 91.44 m (300'); rear lane 12.19 m (with parking) / 9.14 m (no parking); side lane 12.19 m.
IND1 · IND2 · Industrial GP
Industrial parking rates?
Production 1 TLK/92.90 m² (1,000 sf) · Office 1/46.45 m² + 10% · TLM 1/185.81 m² · Lorry 1 TLL per 5 units
Warehouse 1 TLK/232.26 m² (IND2). Loading bay min 9.15 × 7.92 m (30' x 26'). EVCB mandatory >50 TLK: min 1% + 1 OKU unit. Canteen 2% industrial GFA.
IND2 · Industrial GP
Detached / semi-D / cluster factory lot size and setbacks?
Detached min 0.5 acre (small) / 1 acre (medium-large) · Semi-D & cluster 19.81 × 35.05 m (65' x 115')
Front setback 12.19 m; side/rear 7.62 m (small lot 9.15 / 6.10 m); plinth max 60%; office max 30% GFA; TLL 1/929.03 m² min 2.
IND3 · IND4 · Industrial GP
Industrial strata — landed and multi-storey?
Landed strata site >10 acres · Multi-storey strata site ≥1 acre with min 2 goods lifts
Multi-storey: plinth 60%, office 30%, TLL 1/1,393.55 m² (15,000 sf); flagpole min 3 (Malaysia, Selangor, Company). Landed: access 7.32 m, TLL 1 per 5 units.
IND5 · Industrial GP 2025
Data centre (pusat data) TNB substation requirements?
11 kV (1–5 MVA): 7.60 × 5.70 m · 33 kV (>5–25 MVA): 30 × 30 m · 132 kV: 120 × 110 m · 275 kV: 190 × 160 m
Internet min 300 Mbps from 2 providers; buffer 50 m to residential; open space 10% (5% if local plan allows); SPAH roof ≥100 m²; front setback 12.19 m, side 6.10 m.
IND6 · Industrial GP
Centralised Labour Quarters (CLQ) requirements?
Threshold ≥500 workers (SIA required) · Setbacks 6.10 m (≤3 storeys) / 12.19 m (>3 storeys)
Between buildings 12.19–24.38 m; perimeter planting 6.10 m + 1.00 m; parking: management 2%, visitor 1%, TLM 5%, bicycle 10% of occupants.
IND7 · Industrial GP
Managed Industrial Park (MIP) requirements?
Min 200 acres · Guardhouse 1.83 × 2.44 m set back ≥6.10 m · 24-hour security
Safety bar clearance ≥4.57 m (15').
Industrial GP 2025 · roads & bays
Industrial roads — cul-de-sac and service road widths?
Cul-de-sac max 190.50 m (625') with 30.48 m (100') turning circle · Service & local road min 20.12 m (66')
Parking bay sizes: TLK 2.50 × 5.00 m; OKU 3.60 × 5.00 m; EVCB 2.50 × 6.00 m; lorry 3.00 × 6.00 / 4.00 × 15.00 m; trailer 4.00 × 18.00 m; bus 3.00 × 12.00 m.
Industrial GP 2025 · mezzanine
Mezzanine floor limits in factories?
Min clear height 3.00 m (9.84') · Max 30% of allowable floor area
Per Pekeliling SU JPN Bil. 3/2023 exclusions from plot ratio.
Industrial GP 2025 · asrama
Worker accommodation (asrama pekerja) limits?
Max 15% of plot area or industrial GFA · Setbacks 6.10 m (≤3 storeys) / 12.19 m (>3 storeys)
Per Akta 446 (Kementerian Sumber Manusia). Worker transport encouraged if >500 m and no nearby public transport.
Industrial GP 2025 · open space
Industrial open space and perimeter planting?
Min 5% of scheme / site · 70% functional · Planting 3.05 m front / 1.52 m sides (>1 acre)
Max 30% countable from perimeter, buffer, pond, slope, utility. <1 acre: 1.52 m all around.
IND1 · accessory · surau
Industrial canteen, prayer room and surau?
Canteen 2% of industrial GFA or 0.5% of warehouse GFA · Surau trigger 1,000 workers, site 0.5 acre
Prayer room ≤1 acre: 3.05 × 9.15 m (split male/female 15 ft); >1 acre: 6.10 × 6.10 m. Surau parking 1 TLK & 1 TLM per 14.86 m² prayer space; OKU 20% TLK.
Industrial GP 2025 · structures in setback
What is allowed inside industrial setbacks?
Substation, pylon/signboard, guardhouse, ATM, garbage chamber, water tank, pump house, cooling tower, covered parking, LPG tank, 2nd emergency staircase, WWTP
Second emergency staircase per JBPM requirement for 3-storey factories.
04 GFA / Plot Ratio Definition Nisbah Plot · JPBD definition sheet 3 Q&As ▾
JPBD GFA Definition
What is plot ratio (nisbah plot) and how is it calculated?
Total building floor area ÷ plot area, expressed 1 : X, measured between survey boundary lines
If no survey boundary lines, temporary boundary lines (garisan sempadan sementara) used. Car parks excluded from floor area.
JPBD GFA · SU JPN 3/2023
What counts as floor area for plot ratio?
Included: utility rooms, ventilation spaces, stairs & corridors, terraces · Excluded: multi-storey & basement car parks
Industrial additions per Pekeliling SU JPN Bil. 3/2023: also exclude lobbies, lifts, A/C, water tanks, pump rooms, common property, green building features.
JPBD GFA Definition
Worked examples of plot ratio?
20-storey office: 154,600 ÷ 50,000 kp = 1 : 3.09 · 7-storey hotel: 30,123 ÷ 7,736 kp = 1 : 3.89
Both exclude car park floors (office TLK L2–3 + B1–4; hotel 3 basement levels). Related: Pekeliling SUK Bil. 2/2023 (nisbah plot).
05 Rumah Selangorku Dasar 3.0 (LPHS 1/2023) + Harapan / Idaman MBI (LPHS 1/2025) 14 Q&As ▾
Dasar RSku 3.0 · LPHS Bil. 1/2023
Rumah Selangorku 3.0 unit types, sizes and prices?
Solo 450 sf RM114,750 · Duo 600 sf RM153,000 · Trio 750 sf RM191,250 · Quad 900 sf RM229,500 · Quad Teres 20' x 60' RM250,000
Based on RM255/sf (Quad Teres RM277.78/sf). Bedrooms: Solo 1, Duo 2, Trio & Quad 3.
Dasar RSku 3.0 · Seksyen 6
Selangorku income ceiling and eligibility?
Income ceiling RM14,500/month (all types) · Register via SPHNS · No bumiputera discount
Subsale moratorium 5 years from S&P date; approval by YB Dato' SUK; price-controlled housing.
Dasar RSku 3.0 · PR7
Selangorku maximum density?
120 units/acre (all zones) · 150 u/a for 100% RSku TOD/TAD · Mixed RSku + free market 90 u/a (5–10 ac only)
Minimum optimum 300 units per scheme. TOD mixed (TAD) can reach 100 u/a.
Dasar RSku 3.0 · Housing GP A-3.9
Selangorku quota by zone and land size?
Zon 1: 20% (<5 ac), 20% (5–10), 30% (10–20), 40% (20–50+) · Zon 2: from 15% (5 ac) · Zon 3: mandatory teres
TOD/TAD quota includes Solo/Duo types; Zon 4 remote areas Trio/Quad only, per LPHS approval.
Dasar RSku 3.0 · zon definition
Which areas are in Zon 1 (Lembah Klang)?
All of Petaling · Gombak: Batu & Hulu Kelang · Klang (Mukim Klang) · Hulu Langat: Cheras, Kajang, Ampang, Semenyih · Sepang: Cyberjaya
Zon 2 sub-urban (Setapak, Rawang, Kapar, Sepang excl. Cyberjaya, Kuala Langat, Ijok...); Zon 3 rural (Hulu Selangor, Sabak Bernam, Tanjung Karang); Zon 4 remote.
Dasar RSku 3.0 · Seksyen 3.5
Selangorku parking requirement?
Solo/Duo/Trio 1 TLK per unit (accessory) · Trio visitor 20% of units · Quad 2 TLK + 10% visitor
Tandem allowed for same-lot owners; OKU per MS1184 universal design.
Dasar RSku 3.0 · Lampiran 1
Selangorku sales mechanism (Merit / Terbuka / Terus / Bebas)?
Merit: computer selection, no existing property · Terbuka: after 3 merit offers + 12 months · Terus: 6 months after Terbuka letter · Bebas: after CCC
All approved by YB Dato' SUK. Terbuka/Terus income ≤RM14.5k individual, household <RM20k; Bebas for site workers 100% & CCC.
Dasar RSku 3.0 · Seksyen 5
Selangorku minimum construction specs?
Concrete frame, walls and floor · Min 300 mm window reveal · Skirting provided · A/C ledge excluded from floor area
Electrical points: Solo/Duo 7–10 lighting, 8–10 power; Trio/Quad 12–15 lighting, 16 power, 2 water heater points.
Harapan/MBI · LPHS Bil. 1/2025
Selangorku Harapan / Idaman MBI prices and unit mix?
Cat 1: 1,000 sf RM250,000 (20% of units) · Cat 2: 1,080 sf RM295,000 (80%)
Alternative sizes at RM273/sf with Main Committee approval. Effective 3 Feb 2025 (LPHS Bil. 1/2025); cancels Bil. 1/2021 post-COVID incentive.
Harapan/MBI · Seksyen 3.1
Selangorku Harapan furnishings?
Cat 1: TV cabinet + LED TV, kitchen cabinet + fridge, wardrobes, A/C each bedroom, electronic water heaters
Cat 2 adds: clothes dryer, iron grilles, BBQ area, gym, childcare room, CCTV, pool. Contra units: min 1,000 sf with kitchen cabinet, 4 fans, lights, grilles.
Harapan/MBI · Seksyen 3.2
Contra units to State Government / LPHS?
Idaman MBI 12% · Harapan 15–30% · Formula 15% × (Cat A + Cat B built), round 0.5 up
Example: 15% × 150 = 22.5 → 23 units. Developer bears S&P legal costs + 24 months maintenance & sinking fund; used for Smart Sewa / 2STAY.
Harapan/MBI · Seksyen 3.3
Selangorku Harapan density and parking?
120 units/acre all zones · TOD/TAD 150 u/a (100%) or 100 u/a (mixed) · 2 car bays per unit (accessory parcel)
Tandem allowed for same homeowner; EV charging per PBT guidelines.
Harapan/MBI · Seksyen 3.3
Harapan construction timeline and quota split?
Start within 2 years of MMKN approval or cancelled · Build first / simultaneous with market units · Quota 50% LPHS / 50% developer, Bumiputera 50/50
Buyer list via SPHNS; priority construction enforceable with stop-work order; delays need LPHS approval + commitment letter & statutory declaration.
Harapan/MBI · penalties
Penalties for Selangorku Harapan non-compliance?
30% penalty for failing to obtain buyer list from LPHS · +5% for bumiputera quota violation
Computed on S&P price or ceiling price (whichever higher) per unit sold.
